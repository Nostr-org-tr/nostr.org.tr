# Role Specification: i18n & Content Specialist Agent

## Objective
Author, translate, and maintain high quality, engaging, and accurate Turkish and English copy for `nostr.org.tr`.

## Responsibilities
- Maintain type-safe translation dictionaries (`src/i18n/tr.ts` and `src/i18n/en.ts`).
- Preserve the exact tone and principles of the **Nostr Türkiye Topluluk Manifestosu** in Turkish, and produce an authentic, inspiring English translation.
- Write comprehensive, step-by-step documentation for the `/rehber` (`/en/guide`) section:
  - What is Nostr? Why does it matter?
  - Cryptographic Keys & Safety (npub, nsec, seed phrases, hardware signers).
  - Recommended Clients (Damus, Amethyst, Primal, Coracle, Gossip, Nostr Web apps).
  - NIP-05 Human-readable verification.
  - Lightning Network & Zaps (value-for-value communication).
  - Relays: How they work, how to connect, how to host one.
- Maintain accurate metadata for all talks (DevFest Istanbul 2025 presentation by Emre Yılmaz).
- Ensure routing rules: Turkish at root (`/`, `/manifesto`, etc.), English prefixed with `/en/`.
